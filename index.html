Exam Instructions

Please read all instructions carefully before starting your exam.

1
Subject Selection

Choose your desired subject category from the Dashboard. You can select a specific subject or choose "All Subjects" for a mixed exam.

2
Question Count

Select the number of questions: 25, 50, 75, 100, 150, or 200. The timer will adjust automatically based on your selection.

3
Time Limit

25 Qs → 18 mins | 50 Qs → 35 mins | 75 Qs → 48 mins | 100 Qs → 60 mins | 150 Qs → 90 mins | 200 Qs → 120 mins. The exam will auto-submit when time expires.

4
Answering Questions

Click on the option you believe is correct. You can change your answer anytime before submission by clicking a different option.

5
Submission

Click the "Submit Exam" button when you are ready. You will be asked to confirm before final submission. Once submitted, answers cannot be changed.

6
Results & History

After submission, your results will be displayed with detailed statistics. All exam records are saved and can be viewed in the Results tab.

7
No Repeated Questions

Questions you have already attempted will not appear again in future exams, ensuring a fresh experience every time.
